Factor Lab — what actually worked in Indian large caps

Quintile long-short factor books on the NIFTY 50 universe, net of 10 bps/trade costs. Research, not investment advice. Read the survivorship warning below before believing any number.

Survivorship warning. The universe is today's NIFTY 50 projected backwards to 2016. Companies that were dropped from the index after poor performance are missing, so every return here is biased upward by an amount this dataset cannot measure. Value & Quality additionally rest on restated statements (Yahoo serves ~4 annual reports; a +3-month reporting lag is applied). Momentum & Low-Vol are fully point-in-time in prices.

Factors are regimes, not constants: the same book can halve or flip between decades. Survivorship, quantified: an equal-weight portfolio of today's 50 constituents, backfilled, returned +20.3%/yr vs the real, investable NIFTY index at +11.4%/yr over the same months. That gap is the bias — no skill involved. Treat every long-short Sharpe here as an upper bound too.
